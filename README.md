One sentence, written by a program: an alien tourist gives planet Earth a one-star review — and still admits the food was excellent.

> One star: Earth charges admission to its own beaches, bans napping in public, and seasons everything with paperwork — but the tacos from the truck with no name made the trip worth it.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). The output is deterministic: the same bytes on every run. The sentence is one joke with a turn — three small alien grievances, then the food redeems the trip — not a list of complaints.
